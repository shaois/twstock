V87 - 20-day research candidate model

1. Single objective: estimate 20-trading-day net return.
2. Compare expected return with concurrent ETF 0050 return.
3. A stock is a research candidate only when all six gates pass:
   - net return after cost and safety buffer
   - expected return above 0050
   - calibrated profit probability
   - reward/risk ratio
   - liquidity
   - complete data
4. No forced Top 5 and no three-stock cap. Every passing stock is listed.
5. Maximum actual holdings is a separate risk-control setting and does not affect eligibility.
6. AI may explain locked model outputs only. It cannot change eligibility, ranking, thresholds, or conclusions.
7. Until independent validation is complete, all passing stocks are labeled research candidates, never official buys.
