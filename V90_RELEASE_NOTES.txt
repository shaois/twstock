V90 - daily 20-session profit-probability ranking

1. Twenty sessions is now only the forecast and evaluation horizon. It no
   longer locks a five-stock portfolio.
2. Every stock with complete data is ranked again after each completed daily
   cache refresh. There is no five- or ten-stock display cap.
3. Primary rank: probability of a positive 20-session return after the 0.6%
   round-trip cost. Tie-breakers: probability of beating 0050, expected alpha,
   expected return, then the deterministic factor score.
   Probability calibration uses the complete historical cross-section rather
   than extrapolating the old top-2.5% candidate sample to all 200 stocks.
4. The existing overheat guard remains visible as an execution warning. It
   does not remove a stock from the ranking.
5. AI remains explanation-only and cannot change probabilities or rank.
6. New live-performance records use the next trading session's open as the
   executable entry price and the twentieth future session's close as exit.
7. V89 locked holdings are not carried into V90.

Research use only. Historical results do not guarantee future returns.
