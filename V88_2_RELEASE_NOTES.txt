V88.2 - anti-chasing entry execution guard

1. Keeps the V88.1 20-session forecast, 0050 objective, factor weights,
   original cross-sectional rank, reliability tiers, and holding-period rules.
2. Adds an entry-only guard. A stock remains in the research rank but changes
   to wait-for-pullback when its completed base-day return is at least 7%.
3. The same wait status applies when the base-day return is at least 5% and
   the close is in the top 5% of that day's high-low range.
4. The guard can only remove current entry eligibility. It cannot promote a
   stock, reorder the model, alter its 20-day prediction, or let AI override it.
5. The UI displays base-day return, close location, entry-block reason, and
   the maximum entry price that preserves the 0.6% cost plus 2% safety buffer.
6. A waiting stock has a 0% current allocation and AI must say no chasing.
